Method: the four pillars come from the classical Chinese calendar (year changes at Start of Spring, month at the solar terms). The time you enter is used as is, with no birthplace correction, and 23:00 to 23:59 counts as the same day. The element balance is a simple count of the eight characters, so treat it as a game board, not a verdict.
